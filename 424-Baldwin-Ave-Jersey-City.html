Exclusive Offering · Corner Mixed-Use For Sale
424 Baldwin Ave
Jersey City, NJ · The Heights

Grid Real Estate is pleased to offer 424 Baldwin Ave, a ±4,725 GSF corner mixed-use building on a 28' x 50' (1,400 SF) lot, zoned RC-3. The four-story building is configured with three 3-bedroom/1-bath apartments over a garden-level retail/office suite, and can be delivered vacant or with the three residential tenants in place — the ground-floor retail suite is vacant either way.

Two of the three residential units are occupied with strong rents; the third-floor unit is leased roughly $750/month below the building's other apartments — an immediate path to upside once renovated. The property carries no rent control, preserving full pricing flexibility going forward, and the vacant ground floor is turnkey for an owner-occupier looking to run a business downstairs while the residential units carry the mortgage upstairs.

Site Highlights
No Rent Control
Unlike much of Jersey City's older housing stock, 424 Baldwin carries no rent-control restriction — ownership keeps full flexibility to mark rents to market at every turnover.
Renovation Upside
The third-floor 3BR (Unit 3) is leased at $1,900/month against the $2,650 rent achieved by the building's other two apartments — roughly $750/month, or about $9,000/year, of upside once renovated. The third-floor unit can be delivered vacant, so a buyer can start the renovation on day one.
Flexible Delivery
424 Baldwin can be delivered fully vacant or with the three residential tenants in place — a buyer's choice between immediate owner-user/renovation flexibility or in-place cash flow from day one. The ground-floor retail suite is vacant either way.
Owner-User Ground Floor
The garden-level retail/office suite is ideal for an owner-occupier — run a business downstairs while three residential units generate offsetting cash flow upstairs.
Separately Metered, Updated Systems
All utilities are separately metered, and the building's mechanicals — including meters, boilers, and furnaces — have been updated within the past three years.

Built for Owner-Users, Investors & Condo Converters
