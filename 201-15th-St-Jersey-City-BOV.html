201-205 Fifteenth Street
Jersey City, NJ 07302 · Jersey Avenue Light Rail Redevelopment Area
Prepared for Mark Schwartz · Vreeland Properties · October 2026

A 7,500 SF assemblage (Block 7105, Lots 5, 6 & 7) in the Neighborhood District of the Jersey Avenue Light Rail Redevelopment Plan. A concept plan by MVMK Architecture lays out a 14-story, 100-unit Type 1A residential building with 740 SF of ground-floor retail and roughly 86,508 SF of gross building area, retaining the historic facades of #201 and #205. Fifteen units (15%) are set aside as affordable. The plan is conceptual and has not been submitted for approval.

We estimate the value of the site at approximately $6.8 million (about $68,000 per door and $79 per buildable SF), within recommended pricing guidance of $6.0M to $7.0M. Market-rate rents are underwritten at a blended $5.25 per SF, supported by SoHo West and Heights buildings within a few blocks of the site. That produces a stabilized NOI of about $2.33M. We target a 5.70% exit cap rate based on the May 2026 sale of Soho Lofts (273 16th Street) at a 5.53% cap.

Value Drivers
Site Highlights
A Full Concept Program in Hand
MVMK's zoning set lays out 100 units at 8 per floor on floors 2–13, with about 85% net-to-gross efficiency. A buyer starts with a defined building rather than a blank lot. Approvals are still required.
Local Rent Support on the Block
Neighboring buildings, including Hudson House, Hudson House West, Radio Lofts, Soho Lofts and One Ten, achieve effective rents of about $3.50–$4.35 per SF on much larger units (715–1,255 SF averages). The subject’s compact units (657 SF average) are underwritten at $5.25 per SF, with per-unit rents below every comp.
Efficient, Lean Operations
The building is weighted to studios and one-bedrooms (72 of 100 units), with minimal amenity space and no structured parking. We underwrite a 35.2% expense ratio with full real estate taxes and no PILOT. A PILOT or abatement would be upside.
Transit-Oriented Location
The site is inside the Jersey Avenue Light Rail Redevelopment Plan area, near the Hudson-Bergen Light Rail and the Downtown and Newport employment and retail base.

Cap rate support. Soho Lofts (273 16th Street; 377 units, built 2018, about two blocks from the site) traded in May 2026 as part of a 22-property portfolio at a reported 5.53% cap rate. We target a 5.70% exit cap for the subject, a 17 basis-point premium to that trade for new-construction lease-up risk. Two neighboring 2026 deliveries, Hudson House West and Radio Lofts, are in active lease-up with about 16% concessions. That supports the 5% stabilized vacancy and the conservative rent positioning.
